## About Us

SUR:IX is a live online music academy based in **Chennai, India**, teaching **Carnatic, Hindustani, and Western music** to students across **11 countries**. We pair traditional, one-to-one riyaz (disciplined practice) with AI-assisted tools that make progress visible, personal, and consistent - never a pre-recorded substitute for real teaching.

## What We're Building

This organisation houses the engineering behind SUR:IX's learning platform - the tools that let a live lesson feel personal at scale.

- 🎥 **Live Video Classroom** — real-time multi-party video teaching studio (Agora RTC-based), purpose-built for one-to-one and small-group music lessons, with in-class tools like a metronome, finger charts, and an AI practice assistant.
- 🤖 **AI Student Support Agent** — a scoped chat assistant handling rescheduling, billing, and platform questions, with built-in escalation to a real human for anything sensitive.
- 🏫 **Learning Dashboards** — student and teacher-facing dashboards for course tracking, trial bookings, and progress.
- 🚀 **SUR:IX Launchpad** — the pathway from serious student to performing/working musician: exposure, guidance, and real opportunities.

## Courses

`Carnatic Vocal` · `Hindustani Vocal` · `Acoustic Guitar` · `Keyboard` — plus certification prep for **Trinity College London**, **Rockschool**, and **ABRSM**.
